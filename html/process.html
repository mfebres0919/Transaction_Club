How It Works

From signed contract to closed

  1. 01

    Contract Received

    You forward the executed contract and we open your file the same day.

    • Every party's contact details gathered
    • You get a single point of contact
  2. 02

    Timeline Mapped

    Every contingency, deadline and closing date laid out and shared with you.

    • Inspection, appraisal and loan dates tracked
    • Reminders before anything comes due

  3. 03

    Signatures Chased

    You close, and your closing gift is on its way to you.

What does a transaction coordinator actually do?

We handle the paperwork side of a sale for you: opening the file, tracking every deadline, sending documents out for signature, chasing what’s missing and making sure the file is complete before it reaches your broker.

When should I send you a new file?

As soon as the contract is executed. Forward it over and we’ll open the file the same day, so no early deadline slips past.

Will I need to learn any new software?

No. We work around the tools and forms you already use. You forward the contract, and we keep you updated by email and text.

How do you keep me and my clients updated?

You get one shared timeline with every key date, plus a heads-up before anything comes due. We only reach out to your clients the way you want us to.

How is pricing handled?

Pricing is per file. We’ll walk you through it on a quick intro call, based on how many files you carry and what you need from us.

How does the closing gift work?

Once a file we coordinated closes, claim your gift on our homepage. Local gifts are hand-delivered the same day, and anywhere else in-state ships within 48 hours.
