How It Works

Getting started is simple

No onboarding marathon and no new software to learn. Three quick steps and your next file is off your plate.

1. Book a Quick Intro Call

A short call with Peter or Jiang to hear how you work, how many files you carry and where things usually get stuck.

2. Tell Us How You Like to Work

Your broker's requirements, how often you want updates and who we should copy. We set your file up around you, not the other way round.

3. Send Us Your First File

Forward the executed contract and we take it from there: deadlines, signatures and paperwork, all the way to the closing table.

Our Process

How a file moves from contract to closed

  1. 01

    Contract Received

    You forward the executed contract and we open your file the same day.

  2. 02

    Timeline Mapped

    Every contingency, deadline and closing date laid out and shared with you.

  3. 03

    Signatures Chased

    We send, follow up and collect every signature, then check each page.

  4. 04

    Closed & Gifted

    You close, and your gift is on its way: same day if local, 48 hours in-state.
